Call Center Sentiment Analysis

The Call Center Sentiment Analysis Dashboard analyzes customer sentiment, call reasons, call duration, customer satisfaction scores, and negative sentiment across different call centers.

The dashboard is designed to help call center management identify the main causes of customer dissatisfaction, compare performance across locations, and understand the factors contributing to negative customer experiences.

## 🎯 Objectives

The dashboard aims to:

* Identify the call center with the highest negative sentiment.
* Identify the dominant sentiment among customers.
* Determine the call center with the highest positive sentiment.
* Analyze the most frequent reasons customers contact the call center.
* Compare call duration across call center locations.
* Identify customers with the lowest CSAT scores.
* Examine the overall percentage distribution of customer sentiment.
* Compare total negative sentiment across call centers.

## 📈 Key Trends Discovered

* Negative sentiment is the dominant customer sentiment category at 42%.
* Very Negative sentiment also represents a significant proportion at 21%.
* Together, negative and very negative sentiment indicate a substantial customer dissatisfaction concern.
* Billing questions are by far the most frequent reason customers contact the call center.
* Los Angeles and Baltimore record the highest levels of negative sentiment.
* Los Angeles also appears as the center with the highest positive sentiment, showing that customer experience may vary significantly across interactions.
* Low CSAT scores identify specific customers whose cases may require detailed review.
* Call duration varies across locations and may indicate differences in call complexity, service efficiency, or customer issues.

## 💡 Recommendations

* Investigate the major causes of negative sentiment in Los Angeles and Baltimore.
* Review billing processes and billing-related communication because billing questions are the most common reason for calls.
* Provide additional training for agents handling high volumes of negative customer interactions.
* Analyze long call durations to determine whether they result from complex customer issues or inefficient call handling.
* Review interactions involving customers with the lowest CSAT scores.
* Introduce sentiment monitoring to identify negative customer experiences early.
* Improve customer communication and resolution processes for common issues.
* Compare best-performing interactions with negative interactions to identify effective service practices.

## 📌 Conclusion

The Call Center Sentiment Analysis Dashboard provides valuable insight into customer sentiment, call reasons, customer satisfaction, and call center performance.

The analysis reveals that negative sentiment is the dominant customer sentiment category, while billing questions are the most frequent reason for customer contact. Los Angeles and Baltimore show the highest levels of negative sentiment, making them important areas for further operational investigation.

The dashboard can help call center managers improve customer satisfaction, reduce negative experiences, optimize call handling, and identify the main operational factors affecting customer sentiment.
